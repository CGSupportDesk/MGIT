OUR STORY

Learning that keeps pace with change.

MGIT Computer Education has been serving learners in Vadakkencherry since 2004. As technology changes how we work, communicate and create, computer literacy and practical digital skills continue to matter.

MGIT's course references span introductory computer applications, office productivity, hardware, programming, accounting, design and newer areas such as artificial intelligence and data analytics.

Our aim is to make it easier for learners to explore the skills they want to develop, understand their course options and take the next step with confidence.
